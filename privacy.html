Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Outreach is a personal tool operated by its owner to email clinics about personal training services. This policy explains what data it uses and how.

Google account data

Outreach connects only to the owner's own Gmail account, with the owner's permission. It uses that access to:

  • Send outreach emails and follow-ups from the owner's account
  • Create drafts for the owner to review before sending
  • Read the email threads it started, to detect replies, bounces and opt-out requests

It does not read, store or process any other emails in the account.

Recipient data

Outreach stores a list of business contacts (clinic name, business email address, contact name if known, and the status of each conversation). This list is kept locally on the owner's computer. It is never sold, shared or used for any purpose other than this outreach.

AI processing

Business details from the contact list (such as clinic name and type) may be sent to an AI service to help write a personalized opening line. Email contents from Gmail are not used to train any AI model.

Google API Services

Outreach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Opting out

If you received an email from Outreach, reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" and your address will be added to a do-not-contact list and never emailed again.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Outreach's access to Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
