Outreach

Outreach is a personal email tool used by its owner to contact clinics about personal training services for their patients.

It connects to the owner's own Gmail account to send outreach emails, send follow-ups in the same conversation, and check whether a recipient has replied so follow-ups stop. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

If you received an email from this tool and want no further contact, reply "no thanks" and you will not be emailed again.
